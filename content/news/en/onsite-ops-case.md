With AI workloads running flat out, many companies share the same problem: millions of yuan of compute sits in the machine room, and nobody on staff can look after it. When something fails, response is slow and improvised — at best the business stalls and the GPUs idle, at worst it means penalties and direct loss.

This is a real engagement: how COREFIX PRO used a three-part on-site model — **response + operations + enablement** — to move a Guangzhou company from firefighting to prevention.

## Background: the O&M gap

The customer is a Guangzhou-based AI company whose core cluster runs production workloads. Four problems kept recurring:

### 1 · No in-house O&M capability

There was no experienced team on site. Incidents either waited for temporary help or were handled by whoever was free, with the business exposed throughout.

### 2 · No preventive management

Equipment health was left to chance. No scheduled inspection, no performance tuning, no risk forecast — degraded hardware and latent faults went unnoticed until they became failures.

### 3 · Frequent faults, business and compliance pressure

Repeated outages delayed project delivery and risked contractual penalties, with the associated financial exposure.

### 4 · A specialist crew, on site

The customer asked COREFIX PRO to take over day-to-day operations — not just to fix today's faults, but to build a stable, self-sufficient compute operation over time.

## The three-part model: respond, operate, enable

COREFIX PRO deployed **two senior engineers with multi-vendor qualifications** on a long-term basis, and rebuilt the machine-room operating model around three tracks.

### 1 · Fast response: compress every stage

A standard incident process with hard time gates:

- **Within 30 minutes** — ticket accepted, fault assessed, a diagnostic plan issued.
- **Within 2 hours** — engineer on site, fault located and root cause established.
- **Within 8 hours** — parts replaced and system re-tested, downtime kept to a minimum.

### 2 · Preventive operations: from reaction to prevention

The old "fix it when it breaks" habit was replaced with a standing routine:

- **Weekly inspection** — temperature, power draw, compute utilisation and logs, looking for early signs.
- **Monthly review** — a written O&M report with tuning recommendations and risk notes.
- **Transparent handling** — repairs priced per visit, faulty parts shipped only after customer approval, every step traceable.

### 3 · Enablement: teach the team to fish

Service does not mean dependency. COREFIX PRO runs on-site practical training — equipment care, fault identification, first-line diagnosis, routine maintenance — hands-on, so the customer's own staff gradually take over inspection and simple repairs.

## Results

After the engagement the machine room looked different, and the numbers were auditable:

- **200+ tickets** handled, avoiding penalty exposure from outages — roughly **RMB 300,000** of loss avoided.
- **Fault rate down 60%**, average time-to-repair under **4 hours**, a noticeably steadier cluster.
- **In-house capability built**: the customer's staff now run daily inspection and handle simple faults independently.
