## A new chapter for compute services in Shanghai

Shanghai COREFIX PRO Information Technology has officially opened, with professional GPU repair, maintenance and tuning as its core business — a new source of support for China's compute industry. The opening ceremony featured a traditional lion dance, a ribbon cutting and a full house of partners and customers.

![](/news/lingang-center-opening/img-01.jpg)

![](/news/lingang-center-opening/img-02.jpg)

The company focuses on GPU repair and maintenance, technical training and performance tuning for high-end intelligent-computing servers, and has built a cleanroom that meets industry standards. The lab is equipped with X-ray inspection, BGA rework stations and other precision instruments that underpin chip-level GPU repair and fault diagnosis — solving the practical pain points of compute hardware O&M and giving data centres and AI companies full-lifecycle coverage.

![](/news/lingang-center-opening/img-03.jpg)

At the ceremony the company set out its roadmap. Under its motto "集智启航，培优致远" — set out with collective intelligence, cultivate excellence for the long run — it will build a service ecosystem that combines technical service, talent development and solution design. Based in Shanghai with national reach, it will work closely with cloud providers and AI companies to push efficient O&M and technical innovation across the industry.

![](/news/lingang-center-opening/img-04.jpg)

The opening marks the start of a new phase. The company will keep iterating on technology and service, with the aim of becoming a benchmark in compute hardware services and a contributor to the digital economy.
