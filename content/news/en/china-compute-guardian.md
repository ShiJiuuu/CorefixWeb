As AI, big data and cloud computing keep accelerating, GPU servers have become core compute infrastructure, carrying the digital transformation of nearly every industry. For an enterprise, whether that hardware runs stably, how well it is maintained and how fast a fault is cleared directly decide operating efficiency, cost and the ceiling on capacity.

Against a backdrop of fast-expanding compute capacity, scarce specialist maintenance resources and frequent high-end GPU faults, **Shanghai COREFIX PRO Information Technology** was founded in September 2025. The company was set up jointly by **Shanghai Yuanxi Technology** and **Shenzhen Bainai Technology**, combining years of supply-chain resources and hardware service experience from both, and focuses squarely on the after-sales side of compute: one-stop, high-quality equipment services.

As a dedicated compute-equipment service centre, COREFIX PRO covers five core areas — **server maintenance, equipment O&M, GPU performance tuning, hardware repair and industry training**. Unlike repair-only shops, we connect preventive O&M, emergency repair, performance optimisation and engineer enablement into one loop, so a fault is not just cleared but understood and prevented.

## Two companies, one plus one greater than two

Years of work in compute supply chains, hardware development and after-sales maintenance let us merge spare-parts inventory, engineering practice and a large body of field cases. Compared with a single service provider, that means deeper stock, faster response and more proven answers to difficult faults — the **1 + 1 > 2** effect in practice.

Since founding, the company has concentrated on GPU server repair and maintenance. To date we have completed repair and maintenance work on **more than 2,000 H-series servers and over 100 B-series servers**, for customers across AI, cloud computing, large data centres and advanced manufacturing.

We also run a standing review loop: every fault case is written up, common failure patterns across scenarios are catalogued, and compatibility and scheduling issues are folded back into an internal **technical knowledge base**. Practice feeds the method, and the method standardises the process.

## A national service network

Following the national plan for eight compute hubs, COREFIX PRO is building an integrated maintenance network so customers can be served locally and quickly.

Five centres are already operating:

- **Shenzhen** — Greater Bay Area, covering data centres and technology companies across South China.
- **Shanghai Lingang** — the Yangtze Delta hub, serving Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Shanghai.
- **Beijing** — the Jing-Jin-Ji hub, covering North China.
- **Zhongwei** — the Ningxia hub, focused on large data-centre clusters in the Northwest.
- **Horinger** — the Inner Mongolia hub, handling high-volume maintenance and tuning for the North.

Three more are planned — **Chengdu-Chongqing, Guizhou and Gansu** — to complete coverage of all eight national hubs with one standard, one response model and one level of engineering.

## Rooted in Lingang

The core repair centre sits in the **Lingang New Area of Shanghai**, a strategic high ground for compute in the Yangtze Delta. It serves two purposes: shortening the service radius for customers across Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Shanghai — faster on-site response, less downtime, less wasted compute — and filling a gap in the region's compute service ecosystem, supporting Lingang's ambition to become a compute hub with global influence.

## Service as a discipline

Compute is the productive force of the digital economy, and stable O&M is what keeps it running. COREFIX PRO will hold to its principles — **professional, efficient, dependable, improving** — iterating on technology, extending the network and standardising delivery, so that every GPU fleet we look after stays productive for longer.
